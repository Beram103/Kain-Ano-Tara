About the Blog

Real food. Real budget. Real talk.

USC Kain Ano Tara?! is your go-to guide for eating near the University of San Carlos Talamban Campus. We cover everything from sulit budget meals to fast-serve spots for students on the run. Written by students, for students — and everyone else around campus.

Our tone is casual, fun, and honest — like getting recs from a friend who already knows the best spots around campus. Tagalog, English, Bisaya — mixed, because that's just how we talk.
